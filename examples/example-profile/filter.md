# Role filter: Maya Chen

## Seniority range

Most senior considered: Chief Operating Officer / Chief of Staff to a strong CEO at Series C+ scale.
Most junior considered: VP Operations at a Series C+ company. Director of Operations only at a clearly fast-growing scale-up where the next promotion is well-defined.

## Sectors in

- B2B SaaS
- Marketplaces
- Consumer fintech
- Climate and energy transition (open to learn)
- Health technology (open to learn)

## Sectors out

- Tobacco and gambling. Ethical alignment.
- Defence-only. Preference, not absolute.
- Pure crypto / token-issuance plays. Regulatory uncertainty too high.

## Ownership types

- Public: open
- Private VC-backed: open (preferred)
- Private PE-backed: open with caveat that the operating thesis must be growth, not pure cost-cutting
- Listed: open
- Founder-led: open (preferred when founder is operationally engaged)
- Government / NFP: out

## Geography

- London: yes (preferred)
- Other UK: hybrid only
- Amsterdam, Berlin, Paris, Zurich, Stockholm: hybrid with regular London presence
- Singapore, Tokyo: open for the right role with relocation support
- US: not at this stage of family life
- Remote-first: open

## Compensation floor

Total package floor: GBP 280k for UK-based roles. EUR 320k for EU roles. Singapore: SGD 480k.

Note: total compensation including base, bonus, equity. Base alone is not the constraint; the package matters.

## Culture signals I am looking for

- A founder-CEO who has built the company they want to keep building, not someone running out the clock.
- A clear operational thesis. Cost-to-serve, gross margin, payback period — pick two and run them hard.
- Pace and bias for action. Companies where decisions take six weeks are companies I leave.
- Operating leverage as a value, not just a metric.
- A board that engages with operations, not just finance.

## Dealbreakers (hard rules)

- Pure cost-cutting brief with no growth or scale thesis.
- Reporting line below the CEO for COO titles.
- No board engagement for COO titles.
- "Operations" as a euphemism for "make payroll work and stay out of strategy."
- A founder who has not delegated meaningfully to a previous COO. (I will dig.)
- Companies where the previous COO left after less than 18 months without a clear reason.
- Companies that have raised an emergency bridge round in the last 12 months.

## Roles I am most drawn to right now

The right role is a Series C or D B2B SaaS or marketplace where the founder has built strong revenue motion but knows the operating system needs work. Three to five year horizon to either an IPO or a strategic exit. A board that includes at least one operator I can learn from. International expansion already on the roadmap, not theoretical. The job is to build the operating system that makes the next ten times easier than the last ten.
